**Setup.** Open [hound-command.vercel.app](https://hound-command.vercel.app) on a laptop in Chrome. Nothing to install, no account, no cost, and it works offline after one full visit. Press **Open the console**, then **Watch the demo** (about five minutes). The robot code needs Python 3.12, NumPy and a C++ compiler: `python3 robot/sim/cases.py` runs every case and writes the table. Code and setup: [github.com/rishith-c/hound-ultra](https://github.com/rishith-c/hound-ultra) (README).

**The main task:** from a building nobody can see into, to a ranked list of rooms with a safe route to each person, approved by a person. **A good result** confirms or flags every hidden person, never closes a room with a person in it as empty, never calls an empty room a person, and never touches a wall.

**Test cases.** The full case tables (R2 results across cases, R3 failures) are in the project story and in docs/ROBOT_RESULTS.md; `python3 robot/sim/cases.py` reruns all 99 cases, and the console's System check runs the fault drills in the browser.

**Known limits.** **No result here comes from real people or real walls.** The buildings, people, hazards and readings are simulated. The terrain, imagery and map of the valley are real. The network and its numbers are real, measured on simulated signals. **The console is not connected to the rover.** The rover's new code has not run on the rover. The drone is a design and has only flown in the simulator. The extra rovers, beacons, gas sensing and deep scans in the console are concepts, and each one carries a tag that says so. **A still person is the hard case:** 21.4 % on Wi-Fi alone. In the practice building they were never closed as empty, but they were confirmed only 2 times in 20. **The radar and heat likelihoods are assumed design values,** taken from the makers' sheets, not measured. So are the 200 simulated missions in the film. **What breaks the drone:** a motor or gyro failure cuts the motors, and it falls. In the film, the real-wall clip is real video, but its signal is simulated from that video. Not a medical tool, and no substitute for a trained team.

**External dependencies.** three.js, d3, Rapier, Transformers.js and WebLLM load in the browser (vendored or from public CDNs); the in-browser models download once from Hugging Face. No accounts, keys or paid services are needed. No secret credentials are in the repository; the rover key is read from the HOUND_KEY environment variable.
