HOUND brand sheet

The mark is an H made of two walls with pricked tops and one warm dot between them: a person, found. The rings are the signal passing through the walls. Seen another way it is a hound facing you: two ears and a nose.

Rules

  • The dot is always --person. It is the only colour in the mark.
  • Below 28 px use hound-mark-small.svg (no rings, heavier walls).
  • Keep clear space of one wall width on every side. Do not rotate, outline or recolour it.
  • On light backgrounds use the -ink files.
