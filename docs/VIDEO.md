# What to film: Rishith, Mario, Junsu

Film time: about 25 minutes. You only need your faces and voices. All the graphics are done.

## How to film
- Phone **sideways**, back camera, **4K 30** (Settings, Camera, Record Video), **HDR off**.
- Face a window. Nothing bright behind you. Plain wall if you can.
- Chest up, eyes a third of the way down, look **into the lens**. Phone at eye height, on something solid.
- Sound decides everything: quiet room, fan off, phone about an arm away. If you have a second phone, record a
  Voice Memo 20 cm from your mouth at the same time and clap once at the start.
- **Say every line twice.** Wait 2 seconds before and after. Talk like you are telling a friend. No reading voice.
- Learn one line, say it, stop. Then the next.
- Name the files `r1`, `m1`, `j1` and so on, and put them in Downloads. I do the rest.
