**Built during this hackathon.** Almost all of HOUND was built inside the STEM2Connect program (11 October to 29 November 2026):

- **15 November:** the Wi-Fi channel simulator, the 900 people made in Blender, and the neural network trained on them.
- **23 to 28 November:** HOUND Command (all 24 workspaces), the rover and drone code, the practice building with its 99 cases, and the film. This was the same week as the Global AI Builder's Challenge (21 to 28 November), which ran inside this program, and HOUND is entered there too.

**Existed before 11 October:** the rover hardware, its CAD and its first drive firmware (September 2026).
