# The film (2 to 4 minutes)

Judges score the film and the description. Every shot is the running site, recorded frame by frame with `tools/film.py`
(shots in `tools/film-shots.json`), cut in `tools/film_cut.py`. Nothing is mocked up for the camera. A small line at the
end and in the description says what is simulated.

Target 3:05. Voice: Rishith or a teammate reads the script below (plain, calm, no hype). The film also works with the
sound off: each shot carries its own title on screen.

| # | Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Opening title over the valley | After a flood or an earthquake, the first question at every building is the same. Is anyone in there, and where? |
| 2 | 0:12 | The valley film, down the river to Block B, warm light under the concrete | A camera cannot see through a wall. Radar that can costs thousands of dollars. So people are sent in blind. |
| 3 | 0:28 | The idea: the wave field bending round a body | Wi-Fi goes through walls, and a living body changes it. We built a neural network that reads that change. |
| 4 | 0:44 | The training hall, 900 people | We had no recordings, so we made nine hundred people who never existed. Trained on clean signals, it called 81 percent of empty rooms a person. So we trained it on messy ones, with the chip's own faults. |
| 5 | 1:02 | The network, live, one window | 23,372 parameters, every gradient by hand. In simulation, from people it never saw, it finds a moving person behind the wall 86.9 percent of the time. |
| 6 | 1:18 | The rover, exploded CAD, then the real photo | It rides on a rover we built, from parts a school team can buy. |
| 7 | 1:28 | The search: drone, drive in, listen, fan ruled out, person found, route | A whole search, start to finish. The drone maps the site. The rover goes in, stands still and listens. A fan. A hot pipe. Recognised, and ruled out. Then a person. |
| 8 | 2:04 | The proof: evidence adding up to 99 percent | One reading is never enough. Wi-Fi, radar and heat add up, step by step. In 200 simulated missions, all 600 people were found or flagged for a team. No decoy was ever called a person. |
| 9 | 2:20 | The rescue: the plan drawn by the report | A safe route, a break-through point, and a report a team can carry. A person approves every step. |
| 10 | 2:30 | The test wall goes green; then Broken on purpose | Two thousand checks, run in front of you. Cut the radio: it stops by itself. Drop a wall in its way: it plans again. |
| 11 | 2:46 | Fast cuts: drone eyes, inside the rubble, many rovers, the collapse, the horizon | Then we drew what comes next. |
| 12 | 2:58 | Landing title | HOUND. Finding people trapped under rubble, with Wi-Fi. |

End card: "Rover and neural network: built by us. Every search shown: simulated. Built 21 to 28 November 2026."
